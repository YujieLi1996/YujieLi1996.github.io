I am a Marie Skłodowska-Curie Fellow at the Institute of Soil Mechanics and Rock Mechanics (IBF), Karlsruhe Institute of Technology (KIT), Germany, working with Prof. Hans Henning Stutz. My research aims to advance sustainable geotechnical engineering by understanding and improving the interactions between biological processes, geomaterials, and engineering structures.
My work focuses on bio-mediated soil improvement, particularly microbially induced calcium carbonate precipitation (MICP), and its applications in marine and energy geotechnics. I combine field and laboratory experiments, physical modelling, microstructural characterization, and coupled numerical simulations to investigate how bio-cementation alters soil strength, stiffness, and soil–structure interface behaviour. I am especially interested in linking fundamental interface mechanics and constitutive modelling with the performance of offshore foundations, seabed reinforcement, scour protection, and coastal erosion control. More recently, my research has extended to bio-mediated approaches for sand production control during gas hydrate extraction and carbon mineralization and storage in subsea environments.
I received my Ph.D. from Zhejiang University in 2023 under the supervision of Prof. Lizhong Wang and Prof. Zhen Guo. Before joining KIT, I was a Research Fellow at Nanyang Technological University (NTU), Singapore, working with Prof. Jian Chu. 

**Research interests**
- Biogeotechnics: MICP/EICP, bio-cementation, and sustainable carbonate mineralization
- Soil–structure interface mechanics: monotonic and cyclic shearing, bond degradation, and constitutive modelling
- Marine geotechnics: offshore foundations, seabed stability, scour protection, and coastal erosion control
- Energy geotechnics: gas hydrate production, sand control, and subsea CO₂ mineralization and storage
- Multiphysics modelling: coupled biological, chemical, hydraulic, and mechanical processes in geomaterials

I welcome opportunities for academic collaboration and interdisciplinary research.
